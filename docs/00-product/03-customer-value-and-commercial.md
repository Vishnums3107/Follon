# Customer, value, and commercial model

## Initial customer

- Independent systematic traders.
- Technically sophisticated discretionary traders.
- Small proprietary teams and funds with one to five traders.
- Developers replacing a collection of notebooks, spreadsheets, broker terminals, charts, and scripts.

Beginning retail traders are deliberately not the first audience: they need education and support rather than the reproducibility and controls this product is designed to sell.

## Commercial wedge

Sell reliability, control, and research-to-production velocity—not indicators, AI predictions, or promised returns. The early proof is a design partner who can complete normal paper-trading workflows without direct developer help.

## Pricing hypotheses, not launch commitments

| Tier | Indicative position |
| --- | --- |
| Professional | $199–$399/month for one user and one live account |
| Quant | $499–$999/month for advanced data workflows, multiple strategies, and options analytics |
| Team | $12k–$30k/year for multiple users, permissions, approvals, central risk, and audit export |
| Private deployment | $50k+/year for customer-controlled infrastructure, enterprise auth, retention, and support |

Validate willingness to pay with design partners before setting public prices. Treat a seven-figure valuation as a business-quality outcome—retention, recurring revenue, IP ownership, security, operational documentation—not a feature requirement.
